Delete your Family Shield account

App: Family Shield (com.rajeev.familyshield) · Developer: Rajeev Kant · Last updated: 23 October 2026

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

Parent phone

  1. Open Family Shield and tap the menu (⋮) at the top right.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the list, tick "I understand" and tap Delete.
  4. Choose your Google account to confirm it's you. Everything is deleted straight away.

Child phone

  1. Open Family Shield and scroll to the bottom of the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete my account and enter the parent PIN.
  3. Tick "I understand", tap Delete and confirm your Google account.

The parent PIN is needed so the child's protection can't be switched off without the parent. A parent can also delete a child's data by removing that child (see Option 3).

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't use the app (for example, it is uninstalled or the phone is lost), email us from the Gmail address you use in Family Shield:

Email a deletion request

  • To: rajeevkant932@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my Family Shield account
  • Say whether it is a parent or a child account.

We may reply to confirm the request comes from the account owner. Requests are completed within 30 days, and we will email you when it's done.

Option 3: Delete some data, keep the account

  • Remove a child (parent phone): open the child, tap the menu (⋮), then Remove this child. That child's location, places, app list, screen time and rules are deleted and the phone is disconnected.
  • Delete a saved place (parent phone): open the child → Location → tap the delete icon next to the place.
  • Anything else: email us at rajeevkant932@gmail.com saying which data you want deleted. Done within 30 days.

What is deleted

Parent account

  • Your name, email, user ID, parent PIN, plan status and all alerts
  • For every linked child: location, saved places, installed-apps list, daily screen time, rules and the link to your account
  • All pending invites
  • Your Family Shield sign-in account

Child account

  • The child's name, email, user ID and the link to the parent
  • The phone's location, installed-apps list, daily screen time and rules
  • The child's Family Shield sign-in account

What is kept, and for how long

  • Nothing else is kept by us. Deleted data is removed from our database straight away. We have no separate copies.
  • Alerts a child's phone already sent (for example an SOS) belong to the parent's account. If only the child account is deleted, they stay with the parent until the parent deletes their own account.
  • Google Play purchase records are kept by Google, not by us, under Google's own rules. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Your Google account itself is not affected. Only your Family Shield account is deleted.
